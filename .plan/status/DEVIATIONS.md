# Behavioral and compatibility deviation register

**No deviations from locked requirements are approved by this kit.**

| ID | Domain/route | Beta or control expectation | Proposed difference and reason | Alternatives / impact | Evidence | Owner decision | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Record exact fixture/coordinates/state, before/after behavior, affected platforms/routes, why preservation is infeasible, and the smallest alternative. An accepted architectural ADR does not automatically approve a behavioral deviation. Normal changes observed in the vanilla control are compatibility classifications, not permission to change Origins gameplay.
